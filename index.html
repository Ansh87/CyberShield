Step 03 — Cyber-Physical Impact

What could happen in the real world?

The translation layer: the same threat carries different consequences depending on this asset's autonomy and safety criticality. This is the question other scanners don't ask.

⚙ Cyber-Physical Impact Engine

The core of CyberShield: one formula that makes the same threat score differently per asset.
Threat Severity+ Asset Criticality+ Autonomy Level+ Physical-Safety Impact+ Exposure= Cyber-Physical Risk Score

The same cyber threat receives a different risk score depending on whether it affects a laptop, LLM agent, drone, self-driving vehicle, factory PLC, or traffic system. A prompt injection on a chatbot is a data-leak risk; the same class of manipulation on an autonomous vehicle is a collision risk.

About

About CyberShield AI

A cyber-physical threat intelligence prototype — detecting digital, AI, and autonomous-system risks and translating them into real-world safety impact.

Developed by Ansh Saini

CyberShield AI is designed and built by Ansh Saini, a student at South Brunswick High School, New Jersey, as a prototype. It detects digital, AI, and autonomous-system risks and translates them into real-world safety impact.

What it does

The full assessment loop.
1
Asset profilingBuilds a risk profile from autonomy level, safety criticality, and exposure.
2
Threat classificationMaps detected threats to MITRE ATT&CK, MITRE ATLAS, OWASP LLM Top 10, and CISA KEV.
3
Cyber-physical impactTranslates each threat into its real-world consequence for that specific asset.
4
Adaptive remediationUrgency-ordered defensive actions plus relevant security product categories.
5
Human-review reportA shareable incident summary for a person to review and act on.

Core concept

CyberShield AI maps digital threats to asset-specific safety consequences using autonomy level, exposure, physical criticality, and threat class, then generates a prioritized human-review remediation plan.

Technical note

This prototype uses deterministic rule-based threat classification and cyber-physical scoring. Future versions could integrate ML-based anomaly detection, real endpoint telemetry, expanded vulnerability intelligence, and human-reviewed incident-response workflows. It does not perform real malware removal or production-grade detection.

Disclaimer. This prototype uses simulated threat detection and public vulnerability intelligence for educational, research, and decision-support purposes only. It does not remove malware, guarantee containment, or replace qualified cybersecurity, vendor, or incident-response guidance. All findings require human verification.
